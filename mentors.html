Mentor a fellow

We are looking for mentors for a pilot run of the Research Fellowship. Mentors guide one mentee to producing a high-quality piece of conceptual or technical work for the WAISI blog.

This first round is a pilot. We are deliberately running a small cohort, to find out what works before scaling the fellowship up. That makes mentors more important: as well as guiding your mentee, your feedback on the format shapes what the programme becomes.

What a mentor does

You check in with your mentee regularly through the month to provide guidance and accountability, and to steer the project towards a finished write-up. Fellows are expected to be agentic and take the initiative on their own work, so you are not supervising it day to day — you are a sounding board, someone to sanity-check a research direction, and a reason to have something to show each week.

What we ask of you

Capacity
One to two hours per week, throughout October. The fellowship is remote, so your location doesn't matter.
Experience
Experience producing well-argued work within AI safety. We look for at least one concrete example: an in-depth project, a well-made LessWrong post, or previous research experience. Technical experience is not required — conceptual mentors are as welcome as technical ones, and we will match you to a mentee accordingly.

You do not need to be a researcher by trade, or to be at Warwick. Full details of the role are in the mentor brief.

Deadline

Mentor applications close on the 15th of September. Late applications are considered in exceptional circumstances.
