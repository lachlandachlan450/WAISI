Get Involved

Hackathons are open to everyone, and you can check for upcoming ones via our Discord. The research fellowship and the technical bootcamp have a short application so we can keep cohorts small and well matched. If you are unsure if a program is the right fit for you, please err on the side of applying! You can apply at the bottom of this page.

Building Knowledge

Our fellowship and bootcamp require some background knowledge. If you are new to AI safety, consider first completing this BlueDot course, or taking part in an EA AI Safety Fellowship before you apply. Technical experience is not required to complete the research fellowship - you can choose to do conceptual research. We assume that you are comfortable with programming in the bootcamp.

The Research Fellowship

A month-long programme where fellows work on an open problem in AI safety, with support from the WAISI team, and write up what they find. Mentors will regularly contact the fellows to provide guidance and accountability, but fellows will be agentic and take initiative. Fellows can propose their own idea, or work on one of the suggested problems. The fellowship suits people who already have background knowledge on AI safety and want real applied experience. Compute funding is available. See the events page for this term's dates.

The Bootcamp

An intensive programme following the ARENA curriculum: PyTorch fundamentals, transformer architecture, mechanistic interpretability, and reinforcement learning, mixing guided exercises with programming.

You need comfort with programming (Python) and approximately first-year level maths. No machine learning background required.

How to apply

We are currently taking applications for mentors for the research fellowship - more details here. Applications for the bootcamp will be announced later this year.

  1. Fill in the form

    Tell us your background, how much you have interacted with AI safety, and what problem you're interested in investigating.

  2. Follow up

    We'll invite you to an informal conversation, mostly to gauge fit and answer your questions.

  3. Join the cohort

    Offers go out within a week of the deadline, with start dates as shown on the events page.

Deadline

Applications for this term close on the 25th of September. Late applications are considered in exceptional circumstances.
