Apply

Hackathons are open to everyone. The research fellowship and the technical bootcamp have a short application so we can keep cohorts small and well matched.

The research fellowship

A month-long programme where small teams work on an open problem in AI safety, with support from the WAISI team, and write up what they find. It suits people who already have some technical footing and want real research experience. See the events page for this term's dates.

The bootcamp

An intensive programme following the ARENA curriculum: PyTorch fundamentals, transformer architecture, mechanistic interpretability, and reinforcement learning, mixing guided exercises with pair programming.

You need comfort with programming (any language) and first-year level maths. No machine learning background required.

How to apply

  1. Fill in the form

    Tell us your course, year, programming background, and why you're interested. It takes about ten minutes — no CV needed.

  2. Short chat

    We'll invite you to an informal fifteen-minute conversation, mostly to gauge fit and answer your questions.

  3. Join the cohort

    Offers go out within a week of the deadline, with start dates as shown on the events page.
