How can we mitigate catastrophic risk from advanced AI?

The Warwick AI Safety Initiative is a community working on the technical and strategic problems of making powerful AI systems safe. We run a research fellowship, hackathons, and a hands-on upskilling bootcamp.

What we do

Research fellowship

Small teams work on technical or conceptual problems in AI safety over a month-long fellowship, and produce a write-up, building real research experience. Read More

Hackathons

Intensive weekend sprints, including the Apart Research hackathons — a fast way to try a research idea and see it through to a write-up. Read More

Technical bootcamp

An intensive upskilling programme built on the ARENA curriculum: transformer internals, mechanistic interpretability, RL and evals. Read More

Why AI safety

AI capabilities are advancing quickly, and the field of people working to make these systems safe, interpretable and controllable is still small. Applicants with strong conceptual, mathematical or technical backgrounds are unusually well placed to contribute.

You don't necessarily need prior machine learning experience to get involved. See Get Involved for how you can gain the background knowledge you need for AI safety research.
